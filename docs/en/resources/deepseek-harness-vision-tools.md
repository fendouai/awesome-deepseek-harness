## One-liner

## About

**Give your [DeepSeek Harness](https://github.com/deepseek-ai/deepseek-harness) (`dsh`) eyes: keep your text model as the brain, and let a local vision model do the *seeing*. Any text model, any vision model, on Mac or Windows/PC.** The model driving `dsh` is usually **text-only** (DeepSeek, dense Qwen, Llama, Mistral).
