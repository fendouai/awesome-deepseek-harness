## About

Turn a Minecraft Java save into Blender covers and animations. Scans .mca regions for flat open ground, exports the terrain, drives Blender headless with MCprep materials, the BPS rig and three-point lighting, then reviews each render with OpenCV and re-renders until the checks pass.
