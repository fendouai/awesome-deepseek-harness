## About

Deterministic claim support checking for DeepSeek Harness: verbatim quote spans, number matching, labelled-value contradiction, negation and hedging polarity against the cited sentence, REP v1 evidence/claim/verdict records and SARIF output. A paraphrase is reported as insufficient, never as supported.
