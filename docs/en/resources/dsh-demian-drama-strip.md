## About

DSH skill bundle that turns a short drama into a shot-by-shot storyboard and a shootable screenplay. Still frames only ever give composition, so camera moves and subject motion come from ffmpeg 4fps frame-diff sheets, and luma jumps locate cuts and flashbacks. Needs ffmpeg.
