## About

Capture straight into the chat. Click the camera by the composer, park the always-on-top frame over any window, hit Snap, and the framed area lands as an image attachment — no saving, no dragging. The frame stays open: snap again and again and send several at once. The click-through hole keeps the app under it usable.
