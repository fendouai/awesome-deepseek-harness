## About

Gaokao countdown blackboard: desktop blackboard widget counting down days to the gaokao (double-click to collapse into a slim bar), with random knowledge-card quizzes while the agent works; open Markdown knowledge-card framework — drop .md files under subject/category folders to auto-load, with cross-links, favorites, priority subjects and importing your own knowledge base.
