## About

Fixes DSH's silent failure where a tool call never runs and its markup (bare invoke/parameter tags, antml:-prefixed, or a stray 'court') leaks into the reply as text: blocks damaged calls, self-heals in-turn, and after a fork voids the damaged output on the model-visible surface. Zero deps.
