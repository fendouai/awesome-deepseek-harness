## About

在 DSH 右侧栏里提供 SSH 终端、SFTP 文件管理与端口转发的插件；密码交给操作系统密码箱保管，插件自身不实现加密。SSH terminal, SFTP and port forwarding in the DSH right sidebar. Remembered passwords live in the OS credential store; the plugin implements no crypto of its own.
