## About

Plugin-based macro stress testing over MCP. A person names an event; an LLM agent writes the scenario from a corpus of sources, and the person approves it. Models in levels, from the economy to industries and companies, fill in the paths and reprice positions; each model and asset type is a plugin. Output: loss quantiles, ES95, scenario price.
