## 详细介绍

Harness engineering sits at the intersection of context engineering, evaluation, observability, orchestration, safe autonomy, and software architecture. This list focuses on resources that make agents more dependable in real workflows, especially long-running coding and research tasks. Generic agent tooling is out of scope unless the page directly covers harness design, context management, evaluation, runtime control, or other reliability-critical harness primitives.
