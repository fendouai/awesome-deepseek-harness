## 详细介绍

基于 DeepSeek Harness 的 AI 搜索，无广告。三种模式：答疑（摘要+推荐）、百科（可调档位）、浏览（图文视频信息流，相关性/时效性/热度加权）。支持结果语言与搜索历史。AI search based on DeepSeek Harness, ad-free. Three modes: Q&A (summary+recommendations), Encyclopedia (adjustable depth), Browse (feed of articles/videos/images weighted by relevance, recency, popularity). Supports result language and search history.
