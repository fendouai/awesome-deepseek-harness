## 详细介绍

Local, offline speech-to-text for the DeepSeek Harness (dsh). Click the microphone in the composer, speak, and the recognized text is inserted into your draft — transcribed by a RapidSpeech.cpp rs-server running on your own machine. Audio never leaves your computer.
