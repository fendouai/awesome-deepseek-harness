[English](README.md) | [简体中文](README.zh-CN.md) Attention reminders for the DeepSeek Harness Web UI. Whenever a session needs you, three surfaces light up at once: a `(1)`-style badge in the top-left of the frame, a `(N)` count in the browser tab title, and a recolored whale favicon. - **Amber** — sessions waiting for your input (`ask_user` question, approval prompt, plan-mode review). - **Green** — sessions that finished while you were away and have not been opened yet.
