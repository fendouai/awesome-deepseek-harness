## 详细介绍

**The learning & sharing community for DeepSeek Harness (DSH)** Learn the principles · Discover plugins · Share best practices 🌐 **[dshfind.com](https://dshfind.com)** ---

## ✨ 核心特性

- **📖 Learn DSH principles** — structured lessons from the basics up to a chapter-by-chapter deep dive of the Cordis paper: monads, coeffects, revertible effects,
- **🧩 Plugin marketplace** — a live index of DSH plugins, automatically aggregated from the GitHub topic [`dsh-plugin`](https://github.com/topics/dsh-plugin).
- **🏆 Best practices** — plugin development guides, a glossary, and community rankings of authors and projects.
