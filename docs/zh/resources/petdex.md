# petdex

> ⭐ 3,825 · ✅ 活跃 · 插件

## 一句话介绍

A public gallery of animated pets for Codex, Claude Code, DeepSeek Harness, Hermes, OpenCode, Gemini CLI, and more.

## 详细介绍

Petdex is three things working together: 1. **A web gallery** at [petdex.dev](https://petdex.dev) where the community submits, reviews, and showcases animated pets in the Codex sprite format. 2. **A CLI** that installs any pet on your machine with one command and ships them straight into Codex. 3. **A desktop app** that floats a pet on your screen and reacts to your coding agent's activity in real time.
